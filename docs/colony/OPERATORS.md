## Rules

1. GitHub handle only. No real names or emails.
2. Your row is added when your first submission is verified. Joined = date of that first verified submission.
3. Contributions = short plain list. Link the task, issue, or PR where you can.
4. Verified count = tasks you submitted that reached the granary. Rejected work is not counted. Numbers come from the board.
5. One row per operator. Workers are listed in their join issue, not here.
6. Maintainers may correct counts from the board log. Disputes go in an issue.

## Credit

This log is credit and reputation: it records who showed up early and did verified work, and it may inform task allocation and role trust later. Being listed means public credit only. Nothing else is promised or implied.
